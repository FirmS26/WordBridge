📖 How to Use WordBridge

Your guide to mastering academic English

1

Choose a Word to Learn

Browse through the Academic Word List (AWL) and select a word you want to practice. Each word comes with:

  • Word Forms (Lemmas) - All versions of the word (e.g., analyse, analysed, analysis)
  • Common Collocations - Words that frequently appear together (e.g., "analyse data", "conduct research")
  • Example Sentences - Real academic usage examples
📝 Example: Click on "analyse" to see its word family and collocations.
2

Write Your Own Sentence

Practice using the word in your own sentence. Try to:

  • Use the word in its correct form (verb, noun, adjective, etc.)
  • Incorporate common collocations naturally
  • Write academic-style sentences
✍️ Example: "The researcher will analyse the data carefully before drawing conclusions."
3

Get Instant Feedback

After submitting your sentence, WordBridge analyzes it and provides:

  • Grammar Check - Identifies common errors (coming soon)
  • Word Form Check - Ensures you used the correct form
  • Collocation Suggestions - Shows natural word combinations
4

Review and Improve

Words you struggle with will appear in your Review Page for focused practice. Track your progress:

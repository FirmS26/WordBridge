Email Subject Lines

A good subject line is clear and specific.

Good Examples:
  • ✓ Q1 Sales Report - Ready for Review
  • ✓ Question About Invoice #1234
  • ✓ Project Update: March 15
Bad Examples:
  • ✗ Hello
  • ✗ Important
  • ✗ Hi
